IEC Laboratory

The Indirect Evaporative Cooling (IEC) Laboratory was established to advance research in sustainable air-cooling technologies. It serves as a center for experimental studies on thermal performance, humidity control, and energy efficiency of indirect evaporative systems. This page presents an overview of the lab’s structure, operation, and research outcomes.
❮ ❯

What is an IEC?

An Indirect Evaporative Cooler (IEC) uses the principle of evaporative cooling without adding moisture to the supply air. The system relies on a heat exchanger where the working air stream evaporates water and cools the product air indirectly through the wall. This technology can reduce electricity consumption significantly compared to conventional air-conditioning systems while maintaining acceptable indoor air conditions.
