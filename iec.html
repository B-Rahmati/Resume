Lab Setup & Overview

The IEC Laboratory at Semnan University is designed for advanced studies on indirect evaporative cooling systems. The lab includes a variety of sensors, measurement devices, and equipment to study thermal performance and airflow patterns. I established the lab and supervise experiments and calibration of instruments.

Experiments & Research

Experiments conducted in the lab focus on performance enhancement of IEC heat exchangers, airflow analysis, and sensor calibration for reproducible results. Students are guided to perform detailed measurements, analyze data, and link experiments with theoretical models.
